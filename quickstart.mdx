title: "Quickstart"
description: "Connect a server and deploy your first app from GitHub in about ten minutes."
icon: "rocket"
---

You need a Symplax account, a GitHub repository with a web app in it, and a server. If you do
not have a server yet, Symplax can set one up for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [symplax.com](https://symplax.com/signup) and verify your email. The Free plan
    needs no card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a server">
    In the dashboard, go to **Servers** and add one:

    - **Connect your own:** click **New Server**, **Connect existing server**, and enter its IP
      address and SSH login. Symplax signs in and sets everything up. Ubuntu or Debian works best.
      See [Connect a server](/servers/connect).
    - **Buy one:** pick a size and pay by the hour from your wallet. It is ready in about a
      minute. See [Buy a server](/servers/buy-a-server).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect GitHub">
    Go to **Apps** and click to create an app. The first time, Symplax asks you to install its
    GitHub app so it can read your repositories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Pick the repository, the branch and the server, then deploy. Symplax detects the framework,
    or uses your `Dockerfile` if there is one, and follows the build live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app">
    When the deploy finishes, your app is live at its free `https://<app>.symplax.app` address.
    Every push to the branch deploys again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer the terminal? Run `npx symplax login`, then `npx symplax deploy` to deploy and follow
  the build without leaving it. See the [CLI](/cli/overview).
</Tip>
