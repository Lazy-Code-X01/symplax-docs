## How Symplax works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring a server">
    Connect a VPS you already have from any provider with its SSH login, or buy one in Symplax and
    pay by the hour. See [Connect a server](/servers/connect).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy an app">
    Pick a GitHub repository. Symplax detects the stack, builds it, and starts it on your server
    with zero downtime. Every app gets a free `symplax.app` address with HTTPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what it needs">
    A database, environment variables, a custom domain, cron jobs, backups. Each is a few clicks,
    and each runs on your server.
  </Step>
</Steps>
