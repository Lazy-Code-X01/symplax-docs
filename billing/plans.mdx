Your plan decides how many servers and databases you can manage and which features you get.
It is separate from the cost of any server you buy from Symplax, which comes out of your
[wallet](/billing/wallet).

## Compare plans

| | Free | Pro | Team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Servers | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Apps | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Deploys per day | 15 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Databases | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Cron jobs | None | 5 | Unlimited |
| Automated backups | No | Yes | Yes |
| AI failure analysis | No | Yes | Yes |
| Teams and roles | No | No | Yes, unlimited members |
| API keys | 2 | 10 | 10 |
| Custom domains, free SSL, `*.symplax.app` subdomain | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Auto-deploy on git push, live logs | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Community | Email | Priority |

<Note>
  - **Servers** counts every server on your account, whether you connected it or bought it from
    Symplax.
  - **Deploys per day** is a rolling 24 hours, and a failed deploy counts too.
  - Deleting a database frees its slot on any plan.
  - Inside a [team](/teams/roles), limits follow the team owner's plan and count the whole team's
    usage.
</Note>

## Prices

| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | ₦0 |
| Pro | $8/month, or $76.80/year | ₦6,000/month |
| Team | $20/month, or $200/year | ₦15,000/month |

Annual billing is available in US dollars only. Naira plans are monthly. See
[symplax.com/pricing](https://symplax.com/pricing) for current prices.

## Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings** and open the **Billing** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose Pro or Team, and monthly or annual if you pay in dollars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    You are sent to a hosted checkout. When the payment clears, your new limits apply straight
    away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Free plan needs no payment method.

## How you pay

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="US dollars">
    Dollar plans are paid by card through Flutterwave and renew automatically each month or year.
    You get an email 7 days before each renewal.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Naira">
    Naira plans are a one-time payment for one month. Nothing renews on its own, so you pay
    again each month to keep the plan. You get an email 7 days before it runs out, with a link to
    renew.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You get a receipt by email for every plan payment, and your payments are listed in the
**Billing** tab.

## When a plan lapses

If your plan ends without a renewal, you keep your paid features for a 5 day grace period.

<Steps>
  <Step title="7 days before the end date">
    A reminder email that your plan renews or needs renewing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 1 after it ends">
    An email that the plan has expired, with a link to renew.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 3">
    A second email: your access ends in 2 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 5">
    Your account moves to Free.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When your account moves to Free:

- Your cron jobs pause.
- Scheduled database backups pause.
- Every server except your oldest one is locked. A locked server keeps running, but you cannot
  deploy to it or change anything on it until you upgrade.
- Your apps keep running and no data is deleted.

If you own a team, this also applies to the cron jobs and backups on the team's servers.

<Tip>
  Renew at any time, during the grace period or after it. Renewing unlocks your servers and
  resumes exactly the cron jobs and backup schedules that billing paused. Anything you had paused
  yourself stays paused. If you set a new backup schedule while you were on Free, yours is kept.
</Tip>
